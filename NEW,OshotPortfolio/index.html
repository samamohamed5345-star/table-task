03 / END-TO-END PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE

FROM STORYBOARD TO FINAL GRADE.

We don't just show up with a camera. Oshot operates as a turnkey commercial unit—guiding agencies and brands through four disciplined stages.

PHASE // 01↗
PRE-PRODUCTION & STRATEGY

Concept, Script & Storyboarding

Good work starts long before the camera rolls. We translate brand objectives into sharp directors' treatments, visual moodboards, shot lists, and frame-by-frame storyboards so every stakeholder sees the film before day one.

  • CREATIVE DIRECTION
  • NARRATIVE & COMMERCIAL SCRIPTING
  • FRAME-ACCURATE STORYBOARDS
  • CASTING & LOCATION SCOUTING
PHASE // 02↗
PRINCIPAL PHOTOGRAPHY

Cinematography & Lighting Craft

Led by DOP governance, our sets combine 10-bit 4:2:2 S-Log3 cinema acquisition, fast prime optics (35mm / 50mm / 85mm), and intentional lighting design—from soft book-lights to dynamic automotive and gimbal rigging.

  • DOP & CAMERA DIRECTION
  • PRIME LENS PACKAGES
  • CUSTOM STUDIO & LOCATION LIGHTING
  • 180° SHUTTER MOTION DISCIPLINE
PHASE // 03↗
TABLETOP & SPECIALIZED UNITS

Macro Product & High-Key Staging

For consumer brands, luxury packaging, and F&B, we engineer controlled tabletop environments—sculpting specular highlights, liquid dynamics, and microscopic textures that make physical products unforgettable.

  • MACRO PROBE & 90MM OPTICS
  • HIGH-SPEED 120FPS+ CAPTURE
  • REFLECTION & DIFFUSION CONTROL
  • PRECISION PRODUCT STYLING
PHASE // 04↗
FINISHING & DELIVERY

Color Grading, Sound & Hybrid CGI

In post-production, raw logarithmic latitude is transformed inside DaVinci Resolve into signature theatrical color grades, paired with bespoke spatial sound design, 3D product CGI, and multi-format broadcast deliverables.

  • DAVINCI RESOLVE COLOR GRADING
  • S-LOG3 TO REC.709 / PRINT LUTS
  • HYBRID CGI & AI-ASSISTED VFX
  • BROADCAST & SOCIAL MASTERING
